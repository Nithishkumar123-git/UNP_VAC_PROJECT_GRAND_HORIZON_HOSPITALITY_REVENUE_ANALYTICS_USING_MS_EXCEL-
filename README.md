The Grand Horizon Hospitality Revenue Analytics project, completed in collaboration with the United Network of Professionals, focused on transforming hospitality data into meaningful business insights. We performed data cleaning and preprocessing, handled missing values and duplicates, identified outliers, and applied statistical techniques including hypothesis testing, correlation, regression, variance analysis, ANOVA, and A/B testing. We also created pivot tables, charts, and visualizations to understand revenue and operational performance. The objective was to identify important patterns, relationships, and factors affecting hotel performance and support better business decisions. Through this project, we gained practical knowledge of Excel, data analytics, statistics, visualization, and business interpretation.

Most importantly, we learned how to move from raw data → analysis → insights → informed business decisions. The project helped us understand that analytics is not only about calculations, but about using evidence to support better pricing, revenue and operational strategies.
